# Brand Notes

## Mise en place

`MiseLab` is rooted in `mise en place`: the professional kitchen practice of
having everything prepared, organized, measured, and ready before work begins.

This is a strong brand foundation because the product is not just a recipe
viewer. It helps cooks, students, and teachers prepare recipes technically:

- ingredients organized;
- formulas calculated;
- metrics checked;
- costs and yields understood;
- versions and exports ready for production or class.

## Brand Meaning

MiseLab should feel like a calm professional workspace for culinary formulas.
The product promise is preparation plus understanding:

- from recipe idea to technical formula;
- from ingredients to balanced metrics;
- from class exercise to production-ready card;
- from rough import to organized recipe knowledge.

## Possible Messaging

- `Prepare, calculate, and teach recipes with clarity.`
- `A culinary workspace from mise en place to technical cards.`
- `Where recipes become formulas.`
- `Organized recipes. Calculated results. Ready for service.`

## Formula Feature Messaging

`Mise en place for modern culinary formulas.`

This is a strong landing-page or feature headline for the formula/calculation
side of the product. It does not need to cover the entire RecipeHub/recipe
collection direction; recipe browsing, books, imports, and creator features may
need their own messaging later.

## Tone

- Professional, not sterile.
- Educational, not childish.
- Precise, not intimidating.
- Culinary-first, but not locked to pastry only.
